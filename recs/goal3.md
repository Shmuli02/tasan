## 1. Goal 3 wording

**Main goal 3:** *"Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito"*: "Ensure equal and effective cancer care" ([STRAT] p. 3, p. 5, p. 32).

**Target state** ([STRAT] p. 32): *"Syöpästrategian tavoitteena on varmistaa, että Suomessa on yhtenäiset ja vaikuttavat hoitoketjut sekä riittävä ja osaava henkilöstö, jotka yhdessä takaavat yhdenvertaiset ja laadukkaat hoito- ja tukipalvelut kaikille potilaille asuinpaikasta riippumatta."* In English: the aim is uniform, effective care pathways and enough skilled staff, so that every patient gets equal, high-quality care and support **regardless of where they live**.

**The strategy's own diagnosis of the problem** ([STRAT] p. 32): *"Alueelliset erot resurssien ja palveluiden saatavuudessa, hoitokäytännöissä ja tukipalveluissa heikentävät potilaiden yhdenvertaisuutta."* In English: regional differences in resources, access, care practices and support services weaken equality.

**Sub-goals** ([STRAT] p. 32):
- 3.1 National treatment recommendations and care pathways steer equal, **delay-free** (*viiveetöntä*) and effective care (p. 33–34)
- 3.2 A national cancer quality register, systematic review of results, and peer benchmarking (p. 35–36)
- 3.3 Rehabilitation, psychosocial support, health social work and palliative care are part of the pathway (p. 37–38)
- 3.4 Functional-status data guide diagnostics and treatment of older patients (p. 39–40)
- 3.5 Enough skilled staff, including digital solutions (p. 41–42)

The 70% stage I–II share and the 80% screening participation are listed under **Goal 2**, not Goal 3. The roadmap's Goal 3 section ("3. Varmistetaan yhdenvertainen ja vaikuttava syövänhoito") starts on [ROAD] p. 54 (contents p. 5).*

**Target times behind G3-3** ([THL21] section 4.1, printed p. 13 / PDF p. 14):
- *"Syöpäepäilyssä maksimiaika lähetteen saapumisesta ensimmäiseen hoitoon (leikkaus, lääke- tai sädehoito) on 6 viikkoa."* **Referral arrival → first treatment: max 6 weeks.** Very urgent cancers: within a few days, or at most 2–3 weeks from diagnosis.
- Radiology report within **1 week** of the imaging request. Tissue sample taken within **1 week** of the request arriving. Pathology report within **1 week** of sampling (if no special stains are needed). Adjuvant therapy within **4 weeks** of surgery.
- Time points tracked for 16 cancers: referral arrival in specialist care, first doctor visit, date placed in treatment queue, and first cancer treatment ([THL21] Tiivistelmä, PDF p. 5).

The 6-week cancer target is guidance, not law.

> **Caveat (added after the data review):** the THL Ohjaus 21/2016 PDF is watermarked *"Vanhentunut"* (outdated). It remains the reference named in the strategy (p. 5 footnote ***), and no newer target times are published. It has **no lung-specific limit**; cancer-specific limits exist only for breast, prostate and colorectal. Cancer-specific access times are scheduled for 2026–2028 (roadmap 3.1.2, p. 57).

**Linked Goal 2 indicators** (early detection feeds Goal 3 outcomes) ([STRAT] p. 5, p. 30):
- *"Vähintään 70 % syövistä diagnosoidaan levinneisyysluokissa I–II"*: at least 70% of cancers diagnosed at stage I–II, by 2035
- 80% participation in all screening programmes **in every wellbeing services county**
- Referral criteria and operating models included in all national care pathways (p. 30)

**Data caveat stated in the strategy itself** ([STRAT] p. 30): clinical Cancer Registry notifications with stage cover **only about 40% of all cancer cases** (*"kattavat vain noin 40 % kaikista syöpätapauksista"*), and data on first symptom → diagnosis are **not collected systematically**. Any regional stage-at-diagnosis comparison has to carry this caveat.

**Roadmap context on regional inequality** ([ROAD] p. 12–13): the time from first symptom to diagnosis and treatment start currently varies widely by region and by route into care. If the patient cannot push for urgent care, or coordination is poor, the total time *"saattaa venyä erittäin pitkäksi"* (may become very long). Distances can exceed 400 km to the nearest central hospital and 700 km to a university hospital. Outcomes are worse beyond 50 km from the treatment unit (cited study).

**Money** ([STRAT] p. 54–55): Goal 3 (ST3) needs **€51 M over 5 years**, with **€19 M** of projected savings. County-related investments are expected **mainly from 2028**, after legislative changes. The roadmap estimates **€140 M** of extra investment in total ([ROAD] p. 2). Note: the strategy says €156 M ([STRAT] p. 54). The two documents differ, and we report both.

## 5. Implications for our recommendations
1. G3-3 ("treatment within target times") is the Goal 3 indicator closest to what a county can move in one quarter. Its target times already exist (THL 21/2016: 6 weeks referral → treatment). What is missing is **routine regional measurement**, and the roadmap only defines cancer-specific times in 2026–2028 (3.1.2).
2. A county can **pilot roadmap 2.5.2 / 3.1.2 now**: the roadmap explicitly expects "pilotointi ja käyttöönotto käynnistetty hyvinvointialueilla" in 2026–2028 (p. 53).
3. Stage data (Goal 2's 70% I–II) is only ~40% complete ([STRAT] p. 30). Our KPIs should therefore lean on time-to-treatment and process measures, plus Sotkanet screening participation (6103, 3621, 3620), and state this limitation.
