## 60-second summary
**The gap (Goal 3).** Even after adjusting for age, lung-cancer patients in Lapland get curative treatment far less often than in Vantaa-Kerava: **men ≈16.2% (95% CI 12.7–20.4) vs ≈29.8% (24.2–36.1); women ≈23.1% (17.9–29.0) vs ≈39.7% (33.1–47.0)**. The CIs do not overlap. Source: Keuhkosyöpä Suomessa 2019–2023, Kuva 16; the chart was digitised by our data team (±1 pp), so these are not published numbers. The shares are not adjusted for stage or fitness. For men the age-standardised **surgery** share is equal (9.1% vs 9.0%, 2024 report Taulukko 3.7), so the gap is *likely* in curative radiotherapy. That is our inference. Meanwhile, no county can show whether its patients reach treatment within the target time, which is Goal 3's own wording.

**Three actions, in order:**
1. **T3 Tasan clock (Kymenlaakso + Lapland):** the missing public, county-level clock for the Goal 3 target. The county extracts its own referral→treatment dates (baseline in days 1–30, including a registry-completeness check). A day-28 alert goes to a named nurse navigator. Cost: 1 nurse + 0.5 analyst for 90 days, €20,339–€23,988 per county (KT pay data).
2. **T1 Close the missing step (Kymenlaakso):** ~118 lung cancers a year; surgery decisions and operations happen at HUS Meilahti. A week-1 pathway map finds the missing step (3-day CT, reflex biomarkers, pre-agreed HUS slots), and the pilot closes it. KPI: referral→decision ≥7 days faster than the county's own T3 baseline.
3. **T2 Oulu board + curative radiotherapy access (Lapland):** every new case reviewed by the existing OYS MDT. Week-1 gate: the OYS MDT and radiotherapy leads are identified and confirmed, which public sources could not do. A curative-RT access arm (direct referral, reserved SBRT/chemoradiation slots, travel and accommodation) is in the design column, not yet priced. Outcome KPI: age-standardised curative share, with Pohjois-Pohjanmaa (men ≈23.2%, women ≈25.3%) as a **proposed peer benchmark, not a promised effect**.

**Honesty line:** the 42-day reference is THL Ohjaus 21/2016: marked outdated, with no lung-specific limit; cancer-specific times are due 2026–2028 (roadmap p.57). Day-28 and 80% are Tasan design choices. Unsourced costs are given as hours, not euros.

*Optional (Goal 2, secondary, appendix only):* an extra screening reminder for men in Helsinki (65.2%) and Kymenlaakso (65.6%).

## Read the lung numbers carefully
- **Speed and access point in different directions.** Do not tell one "North vs South" story.
- **The 13.5 vs 46-day surgery figure is fragile.** At least 25% of surgical patients in three areas have surgery on the registry diagnosis date. It covers ~1 in 10 patients, runs from diagnosis not referral, covers 2018–mid-2022, and exists only for 5 collaborative areas. **No KPI uses it.**
- **Recording:** drug-therapy coding is implausibly low in Varsinais-Suomi, Vantaa-Kerava, Keski-Uusimaa and Lapland. The MDT-recorded share is a coding artefact (0.1% in Helsinki). T3's first task is a completeness check.


## T3. Tasan clock: county lung-cancer waiting-time tracker with a day-28 alert and a named nurse navigator who acts on it  
*TOP 3 · #1 · Pilot: **Kymenlaakso and Lapland (one per collaborative area); scalable to all 22 areas** · Goal 3: G3-3*

**"This already exists":** Hospitals hold the dates, and some have coordinators: roadmap 1.2.3 is still at 'survey existing models' (R p.32). What does not exist is a public, comparable, county-level clock for the Goal 3 target. Tasan is that missing public clock, not the first navigator in Finland.

**Problem:** Goal 3's own wording, 'Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa' (S p.5), cannot be checked for any county: THL 2017 says national follow-up of cancer patients' access to care 'has been missing', and we found no later public regional series. The only public lung timing is a diagnosis clock for surgical patients in 5 collaborative areas, 2018–mid-2022.

**Action:** Week 1: the county extracts referral arrival, first visit, queue date and first treatment for every lung cancer of the last 12 months. That gives the county's own baseline in days 1–30. From week 5 the dates feed the Tasan dashboard weekly. If there is no treatment decision 28 days after referral arrival, an alert goes to the named nurse navigator. The navigator removes the blocker (test booking, result chasing, MDT slot, HUS/OYS slot) and is the single contact for patient and GP. A monthly board view shows % within 42 days, the median per step, and waits for non-pathway patients (to detect crowding out). The clock tells each county whether its problem is speed (→ T1) or who reaches curative treatment (→ T2). DATA-QUALITY TASK (days 1–30): before any baseline is published, check registry completeness against the EHR. In the 2024 report the age-standardised drug-therapy share is implausibly low in Varsinais-Suomi (0.8% men / 1.0% women), Vantaa-Kerava (3.7% / 3.8%) and Keski-Uusimaa (3.5% / 7.3%) vs ~25% nationally. Lapland is also low (10.0% / 15.3%; CIs below the national level). The likely cause is HILMO coding gaps (data team's inference). So the pilot checks drug, curative-RT and MDT codes, and the no-record cases, against the hospital's own records.

## T1. Close the missing step in Kymenlaakso's lung pathway: week-1 pathway map, then 3-day CT, reflex biomarkers and pre-agreed HUS surgery slots where missing  
*TOP 3 · #2 · Pilot: **Kymenlaakso (Kymenhva; Etelä-Suomi collaborative area)** · Goal 3: G3-3, G3-1*

**"This already exists":** Kymenhva already runs a lung outpatient work-up and a multidisciplinary lung meeting (kymenhva.fi). T1 does not invent a pathway: it closes the missing step that the week-1 map finds.

**Problem:** Kymenlaakso diagnosed 591 lung cancers in 2019–2023, about 118 a year (csv denominator). Its surgical patients depend on another organisation: the surgery decision and the operation are made at HUS Meilahti in Helsinki (kymenhva.fi). **Action:** Week 1: map the current pathway (CT → bronchoscopy/EBUS/biopsy → pathology → biomarkers → Kymenhva lung meeting → HUS Meilahti surgery decision) using T3's dates. ## T2. Every new Lapland lung cancer reviewed by the existing OYS lung-cancer MDT (by video), plus shared access to curative radiotherapy at OYS (RT referral, travel and accommodation, SBRT/chemoradiation slots)  
*TOP 3 · #3 · Pilot: **Lapland (Lapin hyvinvointialue; Pohjois-Suomi collaborative area)** · Goal 3: G3-1, G3-2*

**"This already exists":** Yes. OYS states that the university hospital is responsible for lung-cancer MDTs, surgery and radiotherapy (oys.fi), and Lapland's radiotherapy is given at OYS (lapha.fi). T2 does not create a board: it closes coverage of the existing one to 100% of new Lapland cases, and turns a curative-RT recommendation into a booked slot with travel and accommodation arranged. ## Mismatches between the data and earlier versions
1. **Hero number changed** from the crude curative share (18.5% vs 35.6%) to the **age-standardised, sex-specific** share (chart-digitised). The gap survives age adjustment in both sexes.
2. **v1 led with generic actions** (NSSC, colorectal FIT triage). These are now non-priority. Colorectal is the optional Goal 2 item only.
3. **Speed ≠ access.** Pohjois-Suomi is fastest to surgery and has the lowest curative shares. Men's surgery shares are equal (Lapland 9.1% vs Vantaa-Kerava 9.0%), so T2 now targets curative-RT access, not only MDT review (inference).
4. **The time figure is fragile** (lower quartile of 0 days). It was removed from every KPI, including T1's 46→29 d. T1's KPI is now relative to the county's own T3 baseline.
5. **Granularity.** Times exist only per collaborative area. Kymenlaakso is justified by volume (591 cases in 2019–2023, ≈118/yr) and its dependence on HUS Meilahti for surgery decisions and operations (kymenhva.fi), not by an area median.
6. **"Already exists".** OYS runs lung-cancer MDTs (oys.fi). Kymenhva runs a lung meeting (kymenhva.fi). Tasan closes coverage and missing steps. It does not create boards.
7. **Recording.** Drug-therapy, no-record and MDT shares are contaminated by coding. They are not used to rank counties.
8. **Costs.** Euro figures now come only from KT Oct 2025 pay data plus KT 2026 employer contributions (23.14%). Physician hours, IT integration, CT lists and biomarker panels are given as effort with no euro figure. The roadmap's €300,000 (p.60) is national FICAN/THL peer-review money, not county money.
9. **General-law waiting-time citation removed** from all recs files. Only THL 21/2016 is used as the reference.
10. **Round-2 fixes (19:50):**
    - The OYS MDT and RT leads could not be verified publicly, so they are now a week-1 gate with entry points.
    - The full THL caveat is now in the T2 body.
