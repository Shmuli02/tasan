# Appendix to the Tasan Lapland pilot: T2 partner arm (Oulu MDT + curative-RT access)

> **CONDITIONAL; OYS LEADS UNVERIFIED.** This arm is not part of the one-page ask. It starts only if the week-1 gate below is passed. The main pilot (T3 clock + day-28 alert + navigator) runs fully inside Lapland and does not depend on it.

**Why.** The age-standardised curative share for men is ≈16.2% in Lapland vs ≈29.8% in Vantaa-Kerava (Keuhkosyöpä Suomessa 2019–2023, Kuva 16; values read from the chart by the Tasan data team, ±1 pp). The age-standardised surgery share for men is the same in both: 9.1% vs 9.0% (Keuhkosyöpä Suomessa 2024 report, Taulukko 3.7). So for men the gap is *likely* in curative radiotherapy. This is our inference: the report does not separate curative from palliative RT. Lapland's radiotherapy is given at OYS in Oulu (https://lapha.fi/syopataudit). OYS says it runs the lung-cancer MDTs, surgery and radiotherapy (oys.fi).

**What.** Every new Lapland lung cancer is listed at the *existing* OYS lung-cancer MDT (by video) before the treatment-intent decision. A curative-RT recommendation becomes a booked slot, with travel (Kela) and the OYS patient hotel arranged by the T3 navigator. The registry's "MDT recorded" share is not used as evidence: Helsinki shows 0.1%, so the field is a coding artefact.

**Week-1 gate.** By day 7, the OYS lung-cancer MDT lead and the OYS radiotherapy lead are identified and confirm in writing that they will take part (listing rule + video slot). **If they have not confirmed by day 14, STOP this arm** and continue with T3 only.

**Cost.** €3,518–4,486 for 90 days: an MDT list coordinator nurse at 0.3 FTE (KT pay Oct 2025, €3,174–4,048 a month, × 1.2314 employer contributions in 2026). Physician MDT time, the baseline audit, the video/PACS link and RT capacity are not priced, because we have no sourced figure for them.

**Day-90 stop/go** (Tasan design choices). Go if all of these hold:
- The gate was passed.
- ≥90% of new Lapland cases were listed before the decision (or ≥20 pp above the week-1 baseline).
- The median from histology to MDT is ≤7 days, and listing adds no more than 7 days.
- ECOG/frailty fields are filled in ≥80% of cases.
- A sized RT-access proposal is agreed with the OYS RT lead.

Stop if the leads have not confirmed by day 14, if coverage is <70%, or if listing adds >14 days to the median. The curative share cannot be judged at day 90 because of registry lag.

**THL caveat.** Waiting-time references follow THL Ohjaus 21/2016 (6 weeks), which is marked outdated and has no lung-specific limit.
