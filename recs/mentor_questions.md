## Top 5 for Kaisa Kaitila (AZ Medical Affairs, on site today)

1. **Real lung times, and whether anyone measures the 42-day target.** The only public lung timing is the median from diagnosis to surgery by collaborative area (13.5 d North vs 46.0 d South, Keuhkosyöpä Suomessa 2024, Taulukko 4.1). It is fragile: in three areas at least 25% of surgical patients have surgery on the registry diagnosis date, and it covers only ~1 in 10 patients. Do you know of county-level lung **referral→treatment** times (e.g. Kymenlaakso, Lapland), from a hospital audit, FICAN or an AZ-supported study? Does any county measure the share treated within THL 21/2016's 6 weeks for lung? We note the document is marked outdated and has no lung-specific limit. `bottleneck: diagnosis→treatment` `validates: T3 baseline, T1 KPI (G3-3)`
2. **Is Lapland's gap in curative radiotherapy?** Age-standardised curative share (2026 report, Kuva 16, digitised by us): Lapland men ≈16.2% vs Vantaa-Kerava ≈29.8%, women ≈23.1% vs ≈39.7%. Yet the age-standardised surgery share for men is equal (9.1% vs 9.0%, 2024 report Taulukko 3.7). From your clinical contacts, is the difference curative SBRT/chemoradiation at OYS (referral, travel, slots), or stage mix, frailty, or how Lapland records treatment? Its drug-therapy coding also looks low (10.0% men vs 25.3% nationally). `bottleneck: diagnosis→treatment` `validates: T2 (curative-RT access) and KPI (G3-1)`
3. **Biomarker/NGS turnaround and reflex testing.** In Finnish NSCLC today, is EGFR/ALK/PD-L1 (or an NGS panel) **reflex-tested by pathology at diagnosis** or ordered by the oncologist later, and does this differ between university and central hospitals such as Kymenlaakso? What is the typical time from biopsy to complete biomarker result, and how much is sent out? The roadmap (p. 88) says some tests are bought abroad and answers are not fast enough. Does AZ have published turnaround data we may cite? `bottleneck: diagnosis→treatment` `validates: T1 (reflex arm), roadmap 4.3.2–4.3.3`
4. **Does Oulu's thoracic MDT already review Lapland's lung cases?** Are new Lapland lung cancers routinely presented at the Oulu University Hospital (Pohjois-Suomi) MDT, remotely or in person? Is surgery/radiotherapy capacity shared, or do most Lapland decisions happen locally? Who chairs or coordinates that MDT? If this already exists, T2 becomes "close the gap to 100% presentation" rather than "set it up". `bottleneck: referral / diagnosis→treatment` `validates: T2 design, owners, cost`
5. **Which county would realistically pilot, and how may we cite you?** Would Kymenlaakso (T1) or Lapland (T2) say yes to a 90-day pilot? Or is another county, e.g. South Karelia (18.9%) or Kainuu (20.8%), already more willing or running a lung fast-track or nurse-navigator model we should build on? Who would own the pilot (chief medical officer, pulmonology head, oncology director)? And how may we quote your input in a public submission, e.g. "AstraZeneca Finland Medical Affairs, oral communication, 14 Nov 2026"? Are there compliance limits on naming AZ or AZ-supported data? `bottleneck: all` `unlocks: pilot county, T1–T3 owners, data transparency`
