# Tasan taglines

Three options, each at most six words. Use the recommended line on the submission cover, social card and brand sheet. None of these is a data claim; every number still needs a fact id and source.

## Recommended

**EN:** Same cancer, different postcode.
**FI:** Sama syöpä, eri postinumero.

Locked hook from the spoken pitch. Four words. Honest, no unsourced claim.

## Option 2

**EN:** The county’s missing clock.
**FI:** Alueen puuttuva kello.

Points at the gap Tasan exists to fill: no public statistic reports the share of patients treated within the THL target times (`nodata_regional_target_compliance`). “County” here means a wellbeing services county (hyvinvointialue).

## Option 3

**EN:** Care, level across Finland.
**FI:** Hoito tasan koko maassa.

Plays on the name (*tasan* = even, level). Aspirational positioning, not a claim that care is already equal.

## Don’t use as a tagline

- Any wording that implies lives saved, deaths prevented, or patients denied treatment (`nodata_lives_saved`).
- Any claim of an existing AstraZeneca–county pilot in Finland.
- The “17% earliest stage” figure.
- Day 28 or 80% as if they were official targets (they are Tasan design choices).
