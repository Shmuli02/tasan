# DATA_GAPS: what public regional data exist for Goal 3 and the lung pathway?

Checked 2026-11-14. "Public" means openly downloadable aggregate tables or report tables/figures. Findata microdata is not public aggregate data and is excluded. The machine-readable copy is `csv/data_gaps.csv`, generated by `scripts/build_data_gaps.py`.

**Bottom line**
- **G3-c (treatment within target times): no public regional compliance figure exists for any cancer.**
- **G3-a (5-yr survival of lung/pancreas/liver): no public regional figure.** The nearest are 1-year lung survival by YTA and bowel/breast/cervical 5-year survival by county.
- **G3-b (mortality): county counts and crude rates only.**
- For lung, the diagnosis → treatment interval is public only by **5 collaborative areas, cohort 2018–mid-2022**. The referral → diagnosis interval is not public at all.
