The idea: the Tasan logo is a spirit level, so the level is the star. Its teal bubble is our character, “Bubble”. Care in Finland is tilted, Bubble slides off-centre, and Tasan is how a county levels it out again. The tone is playful, but every number keeps its “about” and its caveat, and nothing says “lives saved”.
