# JN harsh judging rubric (AstraZeneca "Equal Care Across Finland")
Judging: Sun Nov 15 14:00, AZ Marketing (Hanna Rusila, Laura Aaltonen) + Business Excellence (Joanna Tuormaa). Non-clinical, business-minded judges: they reward a clear "what a wellbeing services county should do Monday" story.

## Gates (fail any = not competitive)
- G1 Every claim has a visible source (on screen and in the deck footer).
- G2 At least one Goal 3 indicator of the national cancer strategy 2026–2035, named and quoted from the strategy PDF.
- G3 Concrete recommendations (who does what, where, by when, expected effect), not just analysis.
- G4 Working live demo (slides alone fail).

## Weighted score (0–5 each)
| Criterion | Weight | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Actionability | 30% | Named county/health-centre action, owner, cost/effort, measurable target, linked to roadmap 2026–2031 | Generic "improve referrals" |
| Insight | 25% | A specific regional gap located at one pathway step (detection / referral / diagnosis / treatment start), with magnitude | National averages only |
| Data quality & transparency | 20% | Sources per number, limitations slide, no invented data, uncertainty shown | Unsourced or synthetic data presented as real |
| Execution | 15% | Demo runs offline-safe in < 90 s, no crashes, backup recording | Localhost dies on stage |
| Clarity | 10% | Decision-maker gets message + action in 2 min | Dashboard tour |

Score = sum(weight × score/5) × 100.

## Usual point losers to check
- Synthetic or "illustrative" numbers not labelled as such.
- AI that just restates charts; it must propose actions tied to evidence.
- No answer to "how would a county start next month, and what would it cost?"
- No link to AZ's "Beyond Junction" follow-up with a wellbeing services county.
