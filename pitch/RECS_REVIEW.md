## The four pitch warnings — how they bind the recommendations

1. **No simple North-versus-South story.** Speed and access run in different directions. T1 is a *South speed* intervention justified by YTA surgery times. T2 is a *Lapland access* intervention justified by the lowest crude curative share, in a YTA that is already fastest to surgery. Leading with "the North is worse" or "the South waits" as a single national fact is a contradiction of our data.
2. **Time-from-diagnosis data cover five regions only.** Any time KPI for Kymenlaakso or Lapland as a *county* is a proxy until T3 measures it. The public clock is diagnosis, 1/2018–6/2022, modality within six months, YTA of residence — not referral, not 2024, not 22 counties.
3. **Curative-treatment shares are not age-adjusted.** T2's 18.5% and T1's 19.3% are crude 12-month treatment-record shares, not 5-year survival. Age-adjusted figures exist in the 2026 report charts but were not extracted (PENDING). Until they are, do not rank counties on this as "worse care."
4. **THL 21/2016 is stamped outdated.** We still cite it, because the strategy itself names it on p. 5. We do not replace it with a number we invented, or with the general specialist-care legal maximum. See "Target we cite" under fix 5 above, and `PITCH_SCRIPT.md` / `JUDGE_QA.md`.
