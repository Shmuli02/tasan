# NAMES AND ANGLES — Junction 2026, AstraZeneca "Equal Care Across Finland"

Three name options, three two-minute story angles for a non-technical decision-maker (a wellbeing services county director), and a recommendation scored against `SCORECARD.md`.

**Rules used here.** No "Polku" or near-synonyms (reitti, väylä, path, trail). No invented statistics: only strategy/roadmap/brief/Tyks figures with a source, or `[NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]`, `[COUNTY A/B]`, `[ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM]`, `[OWNER]`, `[SETTING]` placeholders. Spoken drafts are in English (typical Junction pitch language). Prepare a tight Finnish version for these judges and for a county director; keep the same structure. Word counts are listed under each draft.

Name checks were a **quick web search** on 14 Nov 2026, not a trademark clearance. Residual risk remains.

---

## 1. Three project-name options

### 1. Tasan (recommended)

- **Pronunciation:** TAH-sahn. Easy for Finns (everyday word) and for English speakers (two open syllables, no ä/ö/y).
- **Sense:** Finnish *tasan* means "evenly" and "exactly" — equal care, and treatment started on the target time, not almost.
- **Collision check:** No obvious Finnish health product, app, clinic or sote service under this name. Hits were **Tilipalvelu Tilit Tasan Oy** (accountancy) and **Rakennusliike / Konevuokraamo Tasan Oy** (construction / equipment hire). Not health products. https://www.tilittasan.com/ ; https://rakennusliiketasan.fi/yhteystiedot/

### 2. Valpas

- **Pronunciation:** VAHL-pahs. Clear for both language groups.
- **Sense:** "Alert, watchful" — fits an early-warning view for a county director (one of the solution types in the brief).
- **Collision check:** No obvious *human* health product or wellbeing-services-county app. Disclose, because the word is used: **Eläinlääkärikeskus Valpas Oy** (a veterinary clinic); Haltian "Valpas" IoT (hotel mosquito detection); Valpas personnel services; Kiinteistöhuolto Valpas. Health-adjacent only via the vet clinic, not a cancer-care or county-care product. Higher residual confusion risk than Tasan.

### 3. Säie

- **Pronunciation:** Finnish [ˈsæi.e]. Teach English speakers "SIGH-eh". Slightly harder than the other two because of *ä* and the two-vowel ending.
- **Sense:** "Filament / strand / thread" — the thread of the care pathway, held taut, without borrowing path-words.
- **Collision check:** Quick searches did not surface an obvious Finnish health product, app or clinic of this name (results were generic major health apps, not a Säie product). Residual risk: short Finnish nouns can hide unnoticed marks; this is not a trademark search.

**Rejected after checks (do not use):** Tahti (Länsi-Uusimaa county management/reporting use, plus Ondelfino "Tahti" six-week wellbeing coaching, https://www.ondelfino.fi/tahti); Luotsi (Aava occupational-health remote service, https://www.aava.fi/tyoterveys/etapalvelut/luotsi/); Noste (Terapiatalo Noste); Vire (https://vireapp.fi/); Uoma (Axel Health patient-transfer product, used in Varha); Oitis (booking software, https://oitis.fi/en/); Ehti (too close to Ehtii, an AI booking app, https://ehtii.fi/); Loisto (Loisto Terveys in Oulu); Klaara (too close to the Klara patient-communication app); Kelpo (Kelpo Hoiva measurement product).

## 2. Three story angles

Each angle is written for a *hyvinvointialue* director, in under two minutes. Fill placeholders when data and recs land. Do not speak a placeholder as if it were a finding.

### Angle A — Detection: "It starts before the hospital door"

| Piece | Content |
|---|---|
| **One-line hook** | The inequality often starts before the hospital door: the cancer is found too late. |
| **Gap framing** | A sourced, county-vs-county (or group-vs-group) difference in how late cancer is found — stage, or a close public proxy. Magnitude as `[NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]`. |
| **Pathway step** | **Detection.** |
| **Goal 3 hook (mandatory)** | Do not pass Goal 2 off as Goal 3. On the same screen, show a Goal 3 mittari — usually "Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa" (strategy p. 5), and/or the 10% mortality reduction. Stage I–II "70%" is Goal 2, same page. |
| **"What you do Monday"** | `[ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM]`, owned by `[OWNER]`, in `[SETTING]`, inside 90 days. Sized as a county or health-centre step (not "redesign national screening"). Map to a numbered roadmap action (2.5 earlier detection, roadmap p. 51, or a costed communication line). KPI: stage at diagnosis *and* Goal 3 treatment-start share. |
| **Demo moment** | Open `[COUNTY]`. Flag detection as the step where the gap opens. Click the Monday card. Sources and holes visible. |
| **Closing ask** | 90-day Beyond Junction pilot with this county: one detection gap, one treatment-start KPI, one Monday action, with the county's clinicians in the room. |

**Scorecard risk:** Highest G2 risk of the three. Insight can be strong if the data is sharp, but marketing judges ticking "address Goal 3" may still bounce a detection-only story. Use only if the data team's clearest finding is truly at detection, and even then keep the Goal 3 KPI on screen.

**Spoken draft (~268 words)**

Director, sometimes the inequality starts before the hospital door: the cancer is found too late. Finland's strategy has a Goal 2 target, on page 5, that at least 70 percent of cancers should be diagnosed in stages I to II. We will not pretend that is Goal 3. Goal 3, on the same page, is equal and effective treatment — including patients starting treatment within the target times.

Here is the gap. In [COUNTY A], [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM] of cases are found only after the disease has spread, against [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM] in [COUNTY B]. That difference opens at detection, not at the moment treatment is chosen.

On the same screen you still see a Goal 3 number: your share who then start treatment on time, [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]. We will not leave you with a stage chart and no operational KPI.

This is Valpas — watchful. On Monday you do not launch a new national screening programme. You do [ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM], owned by [OWNER], in [SETTING], within ninety days, with an effort band from the roadmap. You measure stage at diagnosis, and the Goal 3 share who start on time.

I open [COUNTY]. I click the Monday card. Every figure has a source. The brief already says Finland's cancer-care data is thin; we show the holes.

AstraZeneca wants a Beyond Junction follow-up with a Finnish wellbeing services county. We are asking you to pilot Valpas for ninety days: one detection gap, one treatment-start KPI, one Monday action, with your clinicians in the room. Find cancer earlier, and still start treatment on time.

### Angle B — Referral: "The break is between the health centre and the hospital"

| Piece | Content |
|---|---|
| **One-line hook** | Many cancer pathways stretch, and break, between the health centre and the hospital. |
| **Gap framing** | A sourced difference in referral speed or smoothness (time from health centre to first specialist contact, or referrals delayed / returned). `[NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]` for `[COUNTY A]` vs `[COUNTY B]`. |
| **Pathway step** | **Referral** (primary → specialist). This is one of the three bottlenecks named in the official brief. |
| **Goal 3 hook** | Headline mittari: "Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa" (strategy p. 5), repeated under 3.1 (p. 33). Roadmap measure **2.5.2** (standardised referral criteria as part of national care pathways) is costed inside **3.1.1**, annex p. 106 (3.1 = 25 000 000 €). |
| **"What you do Monday"** | `[ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM]` — a template the strategist should fill, but the shape is already in the roadmap: a shared referral checklist, a weekly review of deferred or returned referrals, owned by `[OWNER]` in `[SETTING]`, 90-day target on time-to-specialist *and* on the Goal 3 treatment-start share. Do not invent a euro saving: the roadmap (p. 102) says Goal 3 savings cannot yet be fully estimated. |
| **Demo moment** | Open `[COUNTY]`. Click the step between the health centre and the hospital. Monday card appears with sources. |
| **Closing ask** | 90-day Beyond Junction county follow-up: one referral thread, one treatment-start KPI, one action health-centre leads can start next week. |

**Scorecard fit:** Strong on actionability (the roadmap already named the tool) and on the brief's own bottleneck list. Depends on the data team actually locating a referral gap; do not infer one from national prose.

**Spoken draft (~279 words)**

Director, many cancer pathways stretch, and break, between the health centre and the hospital. The official challenge names that transition as one of three known bottlenecks: referrals that are delayed, or that do not move cleanly from primary to specialist care.

Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy is equal and effective treatment. On page 5, patients are to start treatment within the target times. The roadmap already describes the tool: standardised referral criteria, written into national pathways. That is measure 2.5.2, costed inside the 25 million euros for the Goal 3.1 pathway mechanism, annex page 106.

Here is the gap. From a health centre in [COUNTY A] to a first specialist contact takes [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]. In [COUNTY B] it takes [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]. The difference opens at referral.

This is Säie, the thread of the pathway. On Monday, [OWNER] in [SETTING] starts [ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM]: a shared referral checklist, a weekly review of deferred referrals, and a ninety-day target on time-to-specialist and on the Goal 3 indicator of treatment started within time. We will not invent a euro saving the roadmap itself cannot yet calculate for Goal 3.

I open [COUNTY]. I click the step between the health centre and the hospital. Here is the Monday card. Under every number, a source. Where the data ends, the screen says so.

AstraZeneca has asked for a follow-up beyond Junction with a Finnish wellbeing services county. We are asking you to be that county: ninety days, one referral thread, one treatment-start KPI, one action your health-centre leads can start next week. Equal care is a referral that arrives in time for treatment to start on time.

### Angle C — Treatment start: "You already have a national target" (recommended)

| Piece | Content |
|---|---|
| **One-line hook** | You already have a national target: patients start treatment within the target times, wherever they live. |
| **Gap framing** | A sourced county-vs-county difference in the share (or time) of patients starting treatment within the official window. `[NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]` for `[COUNTY A]` vs `[COUNTY B]`. |
| **Pathway step** | **Treatment start** (after detection and after referral). If later data shows the delay *opens* earlier, keep this KPI and move the "where it emerges" sentence — do not drop G2. |
| **Goal 3 hook** | Direct quote, strategy p. 5: "Potilaat pääsevät hoitoon tavoiteaikojen puitteissa." Footnote: THL, Ohjaus 21/2016, *Hoitoonpääsyn seuranta syövän hoidossa* (http://julkari.fi/handle/10024/131630). Official numeric day-targets were **not** extracted this turn (Julkari PDF blocked). Optional sourced line, not a substitute for THL: Tyks says recommendations call for first treatment no later than four to six weeks after the referral is written, sooner if urgent — https://www.tyks.fi/potilaille-ja-laheisille/hoidot-ja-tutkimukset/syopa/syopapotilaiden-hoitoonpaasy-tyksissa — use only if spoken time allows, and label it as a hospital summary. |
| **"What you do Monday"** | `[ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM]`, owned by `[OWNER]`, in `[SETTING]`, starting within 90 days, with a cost or effort band from the roadmap (Goal 3 funding need 49 730 000 €, annex p. 106). KPI: share starting treatment on time. |
| **Demo moment** | Open `[COUNTY]`. Off-target marked. Click treatment start. Monday card. Source under every number. Holes visible. |
| **Closing ask** | 90-day Tasan pilot with this county as AstraZeneca's Beyond Junction follow-up: one indicator, one pathway step, one Monday action, reviewed with the county's clinicians. |

**Scorecard fit:** Cleanest G2 pass, clearest two-minute sentence for a marketing / business-excellence panel, and the most naturally "Monday" KPI a county can steer.

**Spoken draft (~275 words)**

Director, you already have a national target. Goal 3 of Finland's cancer strategy for 2026 to 2035 is equal and effective cancer treatment. On page 5, one official indicator is a single sentence: patients start treatment within the target times.

Here is the gap. In [COUNTY A], [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM] of patients begin treatment inside that window. In [COUNTY B], it is [NUMBER FROM DATA TEAM]. That is not a national average with no owner. It is a difference between places, large enough to act on.

We looked for where that difference opens: detection, referral, diagnosis, or treatment start. In the data we have, it centres on treatment start — after the cancer is found, after the referral is written. If later data moves that opening earlier, we will say so, and still keep this Goal 3 indicator as the KPI.

This is Tasan: evenly, and on time. It shows a county where you stand on that indicator, where the delay sits, and what to do on Monday. The action is [ACTION FROM STRATEGY TEAM], owned by [OWNER], in [SETTING], starting within ninety days, with a cost band from the roadmap. The KPI is the share who start treatment on time.

I open [COUNTY]. I click treatment start. Here is the Monday card. Under every number, a source. We do not hide what we cannot yet see.

AstraZeneca wants a follow-up beyond Junction with a Finnish wellbeing services county. We are asking you to be that county: ninety days, one indicator, one pathway step, one Monday action, reviewed with your clinicians. Equal care is whether a patient in your county starts treatment on time.

## 3. Recommendation: pick **Tasan + Angle C**

**Pick the name Tasan and the treatment-start angle**, unless and until the data team shows that we *cannot* source a treatment-start (or close THL-defined access) gap. If the sharpest *opening* of the gap is at referral or detection, **keep the name Tasan and keep the Goal 3 hoitoonpääsy KPI**, and shift only the "where it emerges" line (toward Angle B or A). Do not switch to a detection-only story.

### Why this pair, scored as a pitch *design* against `SCORECARD.md`

This is a design score, not a score of a finished demo. Placeholders still have to be filled; a 5 on Insight or Execution is not available until real data and a live app exist.

| Gate / criterion | Design view | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Sources | **Pass, if we keep the discipline** | Official quotes are paged; all magnitudes are placeholders or sourced strategy/roadmap/Tyks lines. Fail later if anyone "fills in" a number. |
| G2 Goal 3 | **Pass (strongest of the three)** | Uses the p. 5 headline mittari in so many words. Angle A passes only with extra care; Angle C passes by construction. |
| G3 Concrete recs | **Conditional** | The skeleton has who / what / where / when / KPI / cost band, but the strategist must fill them. Design is ready; content is not. |
| G4 Live demo | **Not yet** | Builder work still to land. The demo moment is specified tightly enough to storyboard. |
| Actionability (30%) | **Target 5, today a 3** | Angle C asks for a 90-day county action with a roadmap cost band and a national KPI a director already recognises. Becomes a 5 only when the action is named. |
| Insight (25%) | **Target 5, today a 0–1** | The frame is right ("difference between places, located at one step"). There is no magnitude yet. Do not write the conclusion before the data. |
| Data quality (20%) | **Target 5, today a 3 on attitude only** | The script says the holes out loud and refuses invented savings (roadmap p. 102). Still needs real sources on the numbers. |
| Execution (15%) | **n/a until the app runs** | Demo path is: county → off-target → treatment-start click → Monday card → source. That is the 90-second script the builder should implement. |
| Clarity (10%) | **Target 5** | One sentence a director can repeat: *patients in my county should start treatment on time; here is the gap; here is Monday*. Name is short and sayable. |

**Why not Valpas + A, or Säie + B, as the default?**

- **Valpas + A** is the right reserve if detection is clearly the data finding, but it is the easiest way to fail G2 or to spend the first minute on a Goal 2 chart. The name is also slightly noisier (vet clinic and other Valpas companies).
- **Säie + B** is a very strong *second* if referral is where the data opens: the brief names that bottleneck, and roadmap 2.5.2 is already a Monday-shaped tool. The name is a little harder for English-speaking teammates and judges to say under stress. Use this pairing as the planned fallback, not the default.
- **Tasan** works with all three angles (evenly / exactly on time), so we can retarget the pathway step without a rename late on Saturday night.

**First thing the rest of the team should hand this pitch:** a sourced county-vs-county (or equivalent) figure for treatment start or THL-defined hoitoonpääsy, plus one concrete Monday action with owner and cost band. Until those two objects exist, this remains a skeleton, not a winning pitch.
